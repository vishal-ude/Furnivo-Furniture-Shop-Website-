Our Story & Heritage

Designed For Modern Living, Crafted to Last Generations.

At Furnivo, we believe furniture shouldn't just occupy space — it should cultivate serenity, inspire mindful living, and weather time with effortless grace.

Founded in Bengaluru, our studio bridges Scandinavian minimalist geometry with the warmth of tactile organic materials. Every silhouette begins with certified sustainable timbers, sculpted by master craftsmen and hand-finished with zero-VOC botanical oils.

The Furnivo Difference

Our Core Principles

We don't manufacture fast furniture. We build enduring architectural centerpieces crafted with reverence for nature.

Ethical Forestry

Every log of European White Oak, American Walnut, and Ash is harvested from certified FSC forests with zero deforestation impact.

Mortise & Tenon Joinery

We employ age-old Japanese and Scandinavian joinery techniques, ensuring structural rigidity that withstands decades of daily life.

Organic Botanical Finishes

Hand-rubbed with natural cold-pressed flaxseed, beeswax, and vegetable hardwaxes that highlight timber grain without synthetic off-gassing.
