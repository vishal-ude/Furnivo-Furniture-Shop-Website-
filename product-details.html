Architectural Proportions & Heirloom Craftsmanship

Every Furnivo piece is sculpted by guild woodworkers with decades of lineage experience. We construct using traditional mortise-and-tenon joinery, eliminating weak mechanical fasteners to ensure multi-generational durability.

The materials are 100% sustainably harvested from FSC-certified European and North American regenerative forests, treated only with non-toxic cold-pressed natural plant oils.

White Glove Delivery Service

All Furnivo orders above ₹25,000 qualify for complimentary White Glove Delivery. Our certified logistics team coordinates scheduled time slots, provides in-room placement, unboxes the furniture, completes professional assembly, and removes all packaging materials.

Care & Longevity Guide

To preserve natural timber luster, wipe gently with a soft microfibre cloth dampened with warm water. Avoid chemical solvents. Vacuum bouclé upholstery gently using a soft brush attachment.
